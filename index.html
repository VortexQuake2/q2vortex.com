Vortex is a mod for Quake II that adds RPG elements such as character classes, persistent experience and levels, abilities, magic runes, and upgradable weapons to the game.

It is a persistent, self-driven sandbox that opens up as you play.

Play against players, bots, or monsters, locking down an arena, defending against an invasion, or capturing flags. Or, go on a killing spree with a variety of playstyles, ranging from defensive line-of-sight planning to aggressive, high-mobility shooter gameplay against creative opponents.
